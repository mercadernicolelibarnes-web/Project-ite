Paper Boats

A child and her grandfather make paper boats while collecting stories about their changing neighborhood.

Chapter 1: A River of Memories

May spent afternoons with her grandfather beside the river, folding old newspapers into paper boats. Recently, the neighborhood had begun to change: familiar shops closed, new buildings appeared, and several families moved away.

May worried that soon nobody would remember what the street used to look like. Her grandfather suggested that she ask the neighbors to share their memories.

May recorded stories about the old bakery, the community garden, and the first school celebration held beside the river. Some neighbors brought photographs; others shared funny stories they had never written down.

Together, May and her grandfather created a small display at the community center. They placed the paper boats around the photographs, each one carrying a short memory.

The neighborhood continued to change, but people began visiting the display and adding their own stories. May understood that memories could help a community move forward without forgetting where it began.
