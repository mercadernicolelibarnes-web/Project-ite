Silent Footsteps

A tenant hears movement in a vacant apartment and investigates a sound that has puzzled the building.

Chapter 1: The Empty Apartment

Omar had lived in the apartment building for only two weeks when he began hearing footsteps from the empty unit next door. The sound came late at night, followed by a faint tapping.

He told the building manager, who checked the records and confirmed that nobody had rented the unit. Rather than enter alone, they arranged an inspection with the maintenance worker.

Inside, the apartment was empty. The tapping came from a loose pipe, while vibrations from the elevator made an old floorboard creak like footsteps. The building manager arranged repairs and asked the residents to report any other unusual sounds.

That night, Omar heard nothing. He felt a little embarrassed for imagining the worst, but the manager reminded him that asking questions was better than spreading rumors.

The empty apartment was no longer a mystery—just a place that needed maintenance.
