Summer Again

Friends reunite to save the community court where their childhood memories began.

Chapter 1: The Last Day of Summer

Jessa returned to her hometown after three years away and found a notice saying the old community court would be demolished to make room for parking.

She met her childhood friends Marco and Lila, who explained that the town needed more parking but many residents wanted to keep the court. It was where generations had played, practiced, and celebrated together.

The friends collected photographs, interviewed neighbors, and organized a weekend event with games and a display of memories. They also prepared a practical proposal to renovate the court and share its schedule with nearby businesses.

At the council meeting, they listened to concerns about repairs and parking, then presented their plan. The council postponed demolition while it reviewed the proposal.

As the friends watched children play that evening, Jessa realized that coming home was not about finding everything unchanged. It was about caring for what still mattered.
