Chapter 1: The Trail Beyond the River

Ari led a small supply team toward a research station beyond the river. After a storm, they discovered that the wooden bridge had been washed away and the familiar path was blocked by fallen trees.

Instead of rushing forward, Ari asked everyone to stop, check their supplies, and compare the map with the trail markers. They found signs of an older route on the opposite hillside.

The group followed the markers carefully, leaving notes at each turn so rescuers could track their progress. When they reached a steep section, they used a rope and moved one at a time.

By late afternoon, they arrived at the station, where the researchers were safe but running low on supplies. Ari radioed their location and reported the damaged bridge.

The team returned the next day with help. Ari learned that leadership was not about knowing every answer; it was about staying calm, listening, and making careful decisions together.
