Letters to You

A library volunteer discovers unsent letters and searches for the person they were meant to reach.

Chapter 1: The Letters in Drawer Seven

Nina was organizing an old desk donated to the library when she found a bundle of letters tied with a blue ribbon. Every envelope was addressed to someone named Elena, but none had an address.

With the librarian's permission, Nina searched old town records and found a flower shop once owned by a woman with that name. Elena recognized the handwriting immediately. The letters had been written by Daniel, a friend she had lost contact with decades earlier.

Elena explained that both of them had assumed the other wanted to move on. Neither had known how much their friendship had mattered.

Nina contacted Daniel's grandson, who helped arrange a meeting. When Elena and Daniel finally saw each other, they spent the afternoon laughing about old memories and admitting how much time they had wasted making assumptions.

Before Nina left, Elena gave her a flower. Sometimes, she said, the words we keep inside are the ones we most need to share.
