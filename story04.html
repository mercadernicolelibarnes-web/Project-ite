After the Rain

Two siblings repair their relationship while helping their family face a difficult year.

Chapter 1: A House Full of Silence

The rain had been falling since morning. Ella watched water drip from a hole in the ceiling while her brother Marco tried to repair a broken chair. Since their father lost his job, the two had argued about almost everything.

When water began leaking toward their mother's sewing machine, they hurried to protect it. Ella held a plastic sheet while Marco moved boxes and searched for a bucket. For once, they worked together without blaming each other.

After the rain slowed, Marco admitted he felt useless because he could not fix every problem. Ella confessed that she was frightened about their family's future and had been taking her worries out on him.

That evening, they sat with their mother and made a list of household expenses. They planned which school supplies were most urgent and agreed to ask relatives for advice instead of keeping everything inside.

Their problems did not disappear overnight, but the silence in the house did. When sunlight appeared after the rain, Ella and Marco knew they could face the next storm together.
