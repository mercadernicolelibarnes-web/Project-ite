Lost in the Woods

Three friends lose their trail during a hike and must work together to find a safe route back.

Chapter 1: The Compass That Pointed Home

Ben, Aya, and Luis were excited to explore a marked forest trail. They followed a narrow path to take photographs, but when they tried to return, the familiar signs were hidden behind thick leaves.

Ben wanted to keep walking, while Aya suggested they stop and check their map. Luis remembered their guide's advice: stay together, conserve supplies, and signal for help rather than guessing at a route.

They found a safe clearing, used a whistle, and made themselves visible from the trail. Aya compared the map with the direction of the stream, while Ben marked their location on a notebook.

A park ranger heard their signal and guided them back to the main path. The friends thanked the ranger and reported the confusing trail marker.

They still loved exploring, but next time they promised to prepare better and never leave the marked route without a plan.
