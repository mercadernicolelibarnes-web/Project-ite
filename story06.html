Midnight Caller

A late-night phone call sends a radio host searching for the truth behind a warning.

Chapter 1: The Call at 12:03

Sam was preparing the final segment of his radio program when the studio phone rang. The clock read 12:03 AM.

A quiet voice said, “Don't play the recording.” Before Sam could ask who was calling, the line went dead. The caller had mentioned archive file seventeen.

Sam found an old recording of a public meeting about the town's water supply. It revealed a disagreement over a report that had been changed before publication. But Sam knew a single recording was not enough to prove what had happened.

The next morning, he worked with his producer and a local journalist to find the original report and speak with the people involved. They checked dates, requested documents, and gave everyone a chance to respond.

That evening, Sam broadcast a careful report explaining what the records showed and what remained uncertain. The truth mattered, he realized, but so did making sure the truth was supported by evidence.
