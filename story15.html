The Blue Envelope

A blue envelope found on a bus leads a commuter to a decades-old secret between friends.

Chapter 1: A Message Without a Name

Mika found a blue envelope under a bus seat while traveling home. There was no name on the front, only an old library stamp and a date from thirty years earlier.

Curious, she took it to the library. The librarian recognized the stamp and found a record of a reading club that had once met there. One member had moved away after a misunderstanding with the others.

The envelope contained a short message asking the group to meet again and clear up the argument. Mika and the librarian searched old community newsletters and eventually found two of the former members still living in town.

They helped Mika contact the others. At a small reunion in the library, the friends learned that the disagreement had begun with a simple mistake that nobody had asked about.

The blue envelope had arrived decades late, but its message still gave the group a chance to make peace.
