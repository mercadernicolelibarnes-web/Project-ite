Whispers in the Hall

A student investigates strange footsteps in an empty school corridor and uncovers a forgotten musical mystery.

Chapter 1: The Footsteps at Midnight

Every evening, Hana stayed in the school library to finish her assignments. One night, she heard footsteps in the hallway, stopping whenever she stopped moving.

The next day, she told her friend Luis. They returned to the library together and waited until the clock showed 8:17 PM. The footsteps came again, leading them to the locked music room.

Their teacher opened the room with the school guard. Inside, they found an old metronome ticking beside a stack of letters. The rhythm had been echoing through the corridor for years.

The letters belonged to a retired music instructor who had left an unfinished melody behind. Hana and Luis brought the music to the school club, and the students completed it for the next school program.

After the song was performed, the strange footsteps were never heard again. Hana realized that some mysteries were simply forgotten stories waiting for someone to listen.
