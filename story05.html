The Hidden Door

A girl discovers a hidden door in her grandmother's house leading to a forgotten underground garden.

Chapter 1: Behind the Old Bookshelf

Bea was cleaning her grandmother's old study when she tried to move a heavy bookshelf. Behind it, she found a narrow wooden door with a rusty handle.

She called her cousin Nico, and together they told their grandmother what they had found. To their surprise, the old woman smiled. The door had been built by Bea's grandfather many years earlier.

With a flashlight and their grandmother beside them, they descended a stone staircase. At the bottom was a beautiful garden filled with vines, pale flowers, and a dry fountain. A message was carved into the stone: A garden survives when someone remembers to care for it.

They found an old journal with instructions for repairing a small water pump. Over the next few weeks, the family cleaned the garden, fixed the pump, and planted new flowers.

Bea finally understood why her grandfather had kept so many things. Some objects held memories that deserved another chance to grow.
