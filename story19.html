One More Chance

Two former classmates meet at a train station and decide whether to give their friendship another chance.

Chapter 1: The Train Platform Promise

Leah was waiting for the afternoon train when she noticed Daniel standing near the timetable. They had been close friends in school but stopped talking after an argument neither had properly explained.

Daniel recognized her first. He held up a book she had lent him years earlier, its pages marked with small paper tabs.

“I kept meaning to return this,” he said. “And I kept thinking I should apologize.”

Leah admitted that she had misunderstood his words back then and had been too embarrassed to ask what he meant. Daniel apologized for walking away instead of explaining himself.

The train arrived, but neither rushed to board. They talked about the years that had passed and the people they had become.

Before they parted, they agreed to meet for lunch the following week. It was not a promise to return to the past; it was a chance to begin again.
