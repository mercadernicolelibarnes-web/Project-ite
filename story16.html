Broken Strings

A school band must work through an old disagreement before performing together again.

Chapter 1: The Song We Left Behind

Leni had not played her guitar since the school band's last performance. She blamed Paulo, the drummer, for changing their song without asking her, while Paulo believed Leni had refused to listen to anyone else's ideas.

When their music teacher announced a reunion performance, both students considered leaving the band. The teacher asked them to meet and talk before making a decision.

At first, the conversation was awkward. Then Leni admitted she had been afraid her part would be forgotten, and Paulo explained that he had changed the rhythm because the original arrangement did not fit the other instruments.

They tried the song again, keeping the strongest parts of both versions. The rest of the band joined them, and the music slowly came together.

On performance day, they played with confidence. Their friendship was not instantly perfect, but they had learned to speak honestly before a small misunderstanding became a lasting distance.
