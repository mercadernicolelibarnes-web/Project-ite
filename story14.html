Crown of Stars

A village astronomer discovers why the stars vanished and must persuade a guardian to return them.

Chapter 1: The Night Sky Queen

Amara spent every clear night studying the stars from a hill outside her village. One evening, she noticed that an entire group of stars had disappeared from the sky.

Following an old chart, she climbed to a tower hidden beyond the hills. There she met a guardian who had gathered the stars into glass lanterns because he feared the world would forget him once the night sky changed.

Amara listened to his story instead of demanding that he release them. She promised to record his name and the history of the tower so future generations would remember his work.

The guardian finally opened the lanterns. The stars returned one by one, brighter than before, and the villagers held a festival beneath them.

Amara continued her studies, knowing that knowledge and kindness could guide people through even the darkest night.
