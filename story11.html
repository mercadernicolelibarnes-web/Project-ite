Across the Sea

Two cousins sail to an island to deliver a map that could help save an old lighthouse.

Chapter 1: The Lighthouse Map

Rafi and Celia discovered a map inside their grandfather's travel journal. It showed a safe channel through reefs surrounding a small island where an old lighthouse had stopped guiding boats.

They took the map to the harbor keeper, who confirmed that the route might allow repair workers to reach the island safely. With an experienced sailor, the cousins joined a supply trip across the sea.

The journey was rough, and halfway there they encountered a fishing boat struggling with a damaged sail. The crew helped the fishermen secure their boat before continuing.

When they reached the island, the lighthouse keeper used the map to guide the repair team through the channel. The light was restored several days later.

Rafi and Celia returned home tired but proud. Their map had not been treasure in the usual sense; it had helped people find their way safely.
