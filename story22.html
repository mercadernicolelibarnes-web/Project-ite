Homebound

A young adult returns to help at the family store and learns more about the choices her mother has made.

Chapter 1: The Road Back Home

Dani returned to her hometown after spending a year away for training. She expected to rest for a few days, but her mother asked her to help at the small family store.

At first, Dani was frustrated by the crowded shelves, handwritten price tags, and bills tucked beside the cash box. She wondered why her mother had not modernized the store sooner.

Her mother explained that money had been tight and that keeping the shop open had required careful choices. Dani realized she had seen only the unfinished work, not the effort behind it.

Together, they created a simple inventory list, reorganized the shelves, and asked regular customers what items they needed most. Dani also helped make clear signs for the products.

The store did not become successful overnight, but fewer items were wasted and customers found things more easily. Dani began to understand that coming home meant sharing the work, not simply pointing out what needed to change.
