The Golden Map

A young cartographer follows an inherited map to find a forgotten village missing from official records.

Chapter 1: The Mapmaker's Secret

Nico inherited a map from his aunt, a cartographer who loved exploring forgotten roads. Unlike ordinary maps, this one used small golden marks beside wells, gardens, and old stone walls.

Nico followed the symbols with a notebook and compass. The route led through hills and fields until he found the remains of a village that did not appear on modern maps.

He met a farmer who remembered stories of the settlement, which had been abandoned after a river changed its course. The old wells and garden terraces were still visible, though many were overgrown.

Nico recorded the location carefully and checked his measurements before adding the village to his own map. He shared the information with the local history group so the site could be documented respectfully.

The golden map had not led to treasure buried underground. It had led Nico to a place that deserved to be remembered.
