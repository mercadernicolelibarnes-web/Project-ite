Dragon's Oath

A young blacksmith's apprentice befriends a dragon who struggles to control its flames.

Chapter 1: The Dragon Who Feared Fire

Tomas was collecting wood near the mountain when a small green dragon stepped from behind a rock. Its name was Ember, and it was frightened of its own fire.

Whenever Ember became nervous, flames escaped without warning. It had left its mountain home because it feared hurting someone. Tomas understood what it felt like to struggle with something others expected to be easy.

They practiced in a clearing, using a metal frame to direct Ember's breath toward a safe target. Their first attempts were messy, but they learned to pause, breathe slowly, and try again.

A few days later, thick fog covered the mountain road and travelers could not find their way home. Ember carefully lit the village signal beacons, guiding everyone to safety.

The villagers welcomed Ember at the blacksmith shop, where it could help heat metal under supervision. Tomas learned that being good at something did not mean never making mistakes; it meant learning and continuing to try.
