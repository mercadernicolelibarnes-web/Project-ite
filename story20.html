The Spellbook

A beginner magician accidentally brings shadows to life and must learn to guide them safely back.

Chapter 1: The Apprentice's First Spell

Milo had practiced the same beginner spell for days, but the words always seemed to tangle in his mouth. During his first lesson with Master Elowen, he opened an old spellbook and tried again.

A cloud of silver dust rose from the pages. Suddenly, the shadows around the room stretched away from their owners and began dancing across the walls.

Milo panicked, which only made the shadows grow larger. His teacher told him to breathe slowly and observe what each shadow was doing instead of trying to force them to stop.

Milo noticed that the shadows copied his movements. He gently raised his hand, and they raised theirs. He guided them toward the open book, one by one, until they slipped back into place.

Master Elowen praised his patience. Milo had not mastered every spell that day, but he had learned that calm attention could be more powerful than panic.
