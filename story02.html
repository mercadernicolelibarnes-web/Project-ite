The Last Kingdom

A young mapmaker discovers a lost crown beyond the mountains and learns that a kingdom can be rebuilt through courage.

Chapter 1: The Crown of Ashes

Liora was the youngest mapmaker in her village. While repairing an old map, she found a route leading beyond the Ashen Mountains, a place no traveler had returned from in years.

An old messenger gave her a silver compass and warned her that the path changed whenever someone traveled with selfish intentions. Liora packed carefully and began the journey.

In the forest, she met a talking fox who guided her around a river that had swallowed the old road. The mountains seemed to move, but Liora kept drawing what she saw and comparing it with her map.

At last, she discovered a ruined hall where a crown rested beneath a layer of gray ash. A voice asked whether she wanted power or the chance to help her people.

Liora chose to bring the crown home, not to rule, but to unite the villages that had been divided for generations. The crown's light returned, and the last kingdom began again.
