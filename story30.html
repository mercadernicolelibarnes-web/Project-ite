No One Knows

A student receives a scheduled message about a school misunderstanding and chooses to check the facts before reacting.

Chapter 1: The Unsent Message

Rina received a message from an unknown account late one evening. It included a screenshot suggesting that her friend had cheated on a school project. The message urged her to share it before the teacher found out.

Rina felt angry and confused, but something about the screenshot looked wrong. Part of the conversation was cropped, and the time stamps did not match the day they had worked on the project.

Instead of forwarding it, she spoke privately with her friend and asked what had happened. They reviewed their project files and found that the screenshot showed an old discussion about dividing research tasks, not copying another student's work.

Rina informed the teacher about the misleading message and showed the full conversation with permission. The teacher reminded the class to check context before spreading claims.

The unknown account never explained itself, but Rina was glad she had paused. A message can travel quickly; the truth takes more care.
