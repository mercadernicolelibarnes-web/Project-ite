The Glass Clue

A museum intern investigates a missing sculpture and discovers that a mistake in the inventory caused the confusion.

Chapter 1: The Shattered Showcase

Felix was helping prepare a museum exhibit when he noticed that a small glass sculpture had disappeared from its display. The showcase was intact, and the security log showed no unusual entry.

He reported the missing item to the curator and checked the inventory records. The sculpture had been moved for cleaning two days earlier, but the return form had not been completed.

Felix and the curator reviewed the storage list and found a crate labeled with the wrong exhibit number. Inside was the missing sculpture, wrapped safely for transport.

The team discovered that a new label had been attached to the wrong crate during the gallery renovation. No theft had occurred, but the record needed correcting.

Felix helped update the inventory and suggested a second-person check whenever objects were moved. The curator thanked him for following the evidence instead of jumping to conclusions.
