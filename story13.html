Coffee and Kisses

A shy café worker and a regular customer become friends through small notes and shared conversations.

Chapter 1: A Table for Two

Theo visited the same café every Saturday, ordering hot chocolate and sitting by the window to sketch. Mara, the café worker, noticed that he always drew the same street from a different angle.

One morning, she left a small note beside his cup: “You make this street look like a place in a story.” Theo smiled and wrote a reply on the back of his receipt.

Their notes became a quiet tradition. They talked about favorite books, family, and the things they hoped to do someday. When a sudden power outage closed the café early, they sat outside and continued talking without their usual routine.

Theo admitted he wanted to apply for an art course but was nervous about being rejected. Mara encouraged him to try, reminding him that he had already spent months practicing.

Weeks later, Theo returned with news that he had been accepted. He thanked Mara for believing in him and asked if the window table was free. She smiled and said it always was.
