The Final Hour

A newsroom must verify an important story before its scheduled broadcast.

Chapter 1: One Hour to Broadcast

Dani was the youngest assistant in the newsroom when a folder of documents arrived just an hour before the evening broadcast. The papers appeared to show that a public report had been altered.

The editor wanted the team to investigate immediately, but Dani noticed that one page had a different date format and no official reference number. Instead of assuming the documents were genuine, she flagged the inconsistency.

The reporters contacted the office named in the documents and requested the original records. They discovered that one page had been copied from an earlier draft and placed in the wrong folder.

The editor delayed the broadcast rather than publish a claim they could not support. The team continued checking the documents and later released a corrected report with clear explanations and responses from the people involved.

Dani was disappointed that the dramatic story had to wait, but she understood why accuracy mattered more than being first.
