Dear Future Me

Two students exchange anonymous notes in a library and discover that they have more in common than they expected.

Chapter 1: Notes for Tomorrow

Ari found a folded note inside a library book. It read, “What is one thing you hope your future self remembers?” Curious, Ari wrote an answer and placed the note back where it had been.

The next day, another note appeared. The unknown writer asked about favorite music, biggest dreams, and the small things that made a difficult day better.

The notes became a quiet routine. Ari began looking forward to them, even on stressful school days. The writer seemed to understand worries that Ari rarely shared aloud.

One afternoon, Sam, a classmate who often studied nearby, admitted that the notes were theirs. Sam had noticed Ari reading the same book and thought a written conversation would be less awkward than speaking first.

Ari laughed and admitted that the notes had helped make the library feel friendlier. They decided to keep exchanging messages, this time with their names signed at the bottom.

Their friendship grew slowly, built from honest words and the courage to say hello.
