Moonlit Promises

Mira never believed in promises made under moonlight until she met Adrian on the old bridge.

Chapter 1: A Promise Beneath the Moon

Mira visited the old bridge whenever she needed a quiet place to think. One evening, she found Adrian sitting there with a notebook full of poems.

They began talking about school, family, and the dreams they were afraid to share with anyone else. Every Friday, they met on the bridge and exchanged stories about their week.

One evening, Adrian told Mira that his family might move to another town. Mira tried to act happy for him, but the thought of losing their friendship hurt more than she expected.

Before he left, they made a promise to return to the bridge one year later, no matter where life took them.

A year passed. Mira returned to the bridge, unsure whether Adrian would remember. Just as the moon appeared above the river, she heard someone call her name. Adrian stood at the other end, holding a new poem—one written about the promise they had kept.
