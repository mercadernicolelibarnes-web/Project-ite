Footprints in Snow

A guest at a snowy lodge investigates footprints outside a locked cabin and finds an ordinary explanation.

Chapter 1: The Tracks at Dawn

Elise woke before sunrise at a mountain lodge and noticed a trail of footprints leading toward a cabin that had been locked all night. The tracks ended beside the wall, with no marks leading away.

She told the lodge manager, who asked her not to wander outside alone. Together with a guide, they followed the trail from a safe path and examined the snow around the cabin.

The guide noticed that wind had covered an older trail, making the prints appear to stop suddenly. A pair of snowshoes had been stored near the cabin, and the marks matched the lodge caretaker's route from the previous evening.

The caretaker confirmed that he had checked a water pipe and returned by a different path. Fresh snow had hidden his return tracks.

Elise was relieved. She learned that a mystery can look impossible when only part of the evidence is visible.
