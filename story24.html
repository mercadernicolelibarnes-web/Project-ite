Behind Closed Doors

A receptionist notices that a meeting room keeps appearing in the booking system even when it is empty.

Chapter 1: The Room at the End

Bea worked at the front desk of a small conference center. Every morning, room nineteen appeared as booked in the computer, even though nobody had reserved it and the room stood empty.

At first, Bea thought it was a mistake. But the booking returned each night with a different meeting name and no contact details. She recorded the dates and informed the manager instead of trying to access private accounts herself.

A technician reviewed the booking software and discovered that an outdated test account was automatically restoring old sample reservations. The names were not real meetings; they were leftover data from a system demonstration.

The technician removed the test account, corrected the records, and checked the other rooms for similar errors.

Bea was glad the mystery had an ordinary explanation. Her careful notes had helped the staff solve the problem without accusing anyone unfairly.
