The Missing Page

A book club discovers that every copy of a famous novel is missing the same page.

Chapter 1: Page Seventeen

June was discussing a famous novel with her book club when she noticed that every copy jumped from page sixteen to page eighteen. The pages were not torn; page seventeen had simply never been printed.

The group visited a retired editor named Mrs. Bell, who explained that the original page contained a dedication to a nurse who had helped the author through a difficult time. The nurse had asked to keep her name private, so later editions left the page out.

Mrs. Bell still had a proof copy in her archive. With permission, the club examined it and found the missing dedication. There was no secret code or hidden confession—only a sincere expression of gratitude.

June suggested that the library create a display showing the different editions and explaining the page's history. The publisher confirmed the story and approved a reproduction for educational use.

The club learned that sometimes the most interesting mystery is the history of how a story came to be told.
